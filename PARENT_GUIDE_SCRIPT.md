# Parent & Student Guide — voiceover script (for the WhatsApp video)

A ~90-second explainer that tours the student portal one section at a time, spoken in the
**third person** (a narrator describing the portal). Written for a **natural AI voice**
(e.g. ElevenLabs) and addressed to **students AND parents** (no "your child" — some students
are ~16 and will watch it too).

How to make the video: paste the ElevenLabs block below to generate the voiceover, then send
me the new MP3 and I'll rebuild the dashboard walkthrough video (with the matching "Mrs Reem
Shata" cards and a Lesson Espresso scene) timed to it.

---

## ElevenLabs script (paste this whole block in)

Welcome to Mrs Reem Shata's Physics portal. Here's a quick tour of everything it offers, for students and parents.

First, Recordings. You can re-watch every lesson, any time you need to revise. Each video shows the student's name across it, to keep it private.

Right below is Lesson Espresso — short two to three minute summaries that capture the key points of a lesson. Perfect for a quick revision before an exam.

Next, Homework. Every assignment appears here with its due date. Just tap Submit to upload a photo or PDF of your work, and you can always see what's finished and what's still pending.

Then, Attendance. This keeps a clear record of every lesson — present, late, or absent — so it's always easy to check.

In Marks, you'll find every exam with the score, the percentage, and the official grade from one to nine, so progress is easy to follow.

My Progress brings it all together in simple charts — average marks, homework completed, and attendance — easy for students and parents to follow at a glance.

The Class Wall is where Mrs Reem posts announcements and reminders for the whole class.

And the A.I. Study Helper answers physics questions any time, explaining concepts and worked examples from the I-G-C-S-E syllabus.

Everything in one place, whenever you need it. Thank you for learning with Mrs Reem Shata.

## ElevenLabs tips
- "A.I." and "I-G-C-S-E" are written so the voice says the letters clearly. Change them back if your voice already pronounces them well.
- Warm voices that suit this: Rachel, Sarah, or Charlotte. Settings ~ Stability 50%, Similarity 75% for a calm, consistent read.
- Want a male voice or an Arabic version? Just ask (there's also an Egyptian Arabic version in PARENT_GUIDE_SCRIPT_AR.md).
