About

A patient guide through the laws that shape the world.

Eng Reem Shata is a physics teacher with over 20 years of experience in education. She holds a degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from AASTMT and began her teaching journey in 2004.

Throughout her career, she has had the privilege of teaching at leading schools including Riada, EBS, Alexandria International School, IBSA, City International School, Quds International School, EELS, and Pioneers.

Her passion lies in making physics engaging, relatable, and accessible to every student.
